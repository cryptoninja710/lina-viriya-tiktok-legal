QUESTIONS

Before you use the demo

Does this connect to my TikTok account?

No. There is no TikTok login, OAuth connection, token storage or API request on this preview site.

Where are my planner entries saved?

Only in localStorage in the browser and device you are using. Clearing the browser's site data or choosing “Clear all drafts” removes them. They do not sync to other devices.

Can I publish from this page?

No. Use TikTok's official app to publish. API-based publishing is not enabled or approved.

Are the legal pages final?

No. The Terms of Service and Privacy Policy pages are clearly marked as drafts pending confirmation of the service operator's legal identity and jurisdiction. They are not ready to be used as final legal disclosures.
