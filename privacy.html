Lina Viriya Social Agent

Privacy Policy

Effective when published. This policy applies to Lina Viriya Social Agent (“App”), a personal integration operated by the owner of the TikTok account @linaviriya. Privacy contact: linaviriya@proton.me.

1. Current status

The App is in development. It does not currently have a confirmed TikTok authorization or production connection, and it does not currently retrieve TikTok account data. This policy describes the data expected to be processed if the account owner later authorizes the integration.

2. Data that may be processed after authorization

  • TikTok account identifier and basic profile information allowed by the permissions granted by the account owner.
  • Authorization tokens needed to access the approved TikTok API features.
  • Media, captions, and posting settings that the account owner chooses to submit.
  • Responses indicating authorization or content-submission status.

The App will use this information only to provide the account-management features requested by the account owner. It will not sell TikTok account data or use it for advertising.

3. Storage and security

The App is intended to run for the account owner on their own device. Any future TikTok credentials will be stored locally in the owner's Windows user profile with access restricted to that user. Content will be sent to TikTok only when needed for an authorized API action. The App does not currently store TikTok credentials because TikTok authorization is not active.

4. Sharing

When the owner authorizes an API action, the relevant information is transmitted to TikTok through its official API. TikTok then processes it under TikTok's own terms and privacy policy. This static legal-information site is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process ordinary site-visit data under its own privacy statement. No advertising or analytics service is intentionally integrated into this site.

5. Retention and deletion

Because TikTok authorization is not active, the App currently has no TikTok tokens or TikTok account data to retain. Once enabled, the account owner may revoke access through TikTok and remove locally stored credentials and files. Contact the privacy email below for assistance or questions about deletion.

6. Rights and changes

Depending on applicable law, the account owner may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data and to object to or restrict certain processing. Requests can be sent to the contact below. This policy will be updated if the App's data practices change.

7. Contact

Privacy questions: linaviriya@proton.me.
