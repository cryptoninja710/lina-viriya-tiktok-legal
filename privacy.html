2. Information entered in the planner

The planner stores the draft title, caption notes and optional date in your browser's localStorage on the device and browser profile you are using. This information is not sent to this website's operator by the planner code, and it is not sent to TikTok. The entries do not synchronize across devices. Anyone with access to the same browser profile or device may be able to view them.

3. Delete local drafts

Use “Clear all drafts” in the planner to delete all entries for this demo from localStorage. You can also remove the site's stored data using your browser settings. Uninstalling or clearing browser data may also remove them.

This preview does not offer TikTok login, OAuth authorization, API access, or direct publishing. It does not request or store TikTok passwords, access tokens, profile information or uploaded media. Do not enter credentials or tokens into the planner.
